# Support

Thank you for using Talus Process Monitor!

## Community support

- **Documentation:** start with the [README](README.md), the
  [Architecture guide](ARCHITECTURE.md) and the
  [customer activation guide](docs/customer-activation-guide.md)
- **Bugs & questions:** [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/BartoszOsiej/talus-process-monitor/issues)
  — please search before opening a new one
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** open a public issue — follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

## Enterprise support

Customers with an **Enterprise license** get the priority support channel
defined in their purchase record (email to the maintainer). Include your
license ID (from `talus license show`) and never your license key itself.

Licensing questions (seats, transfers, renewals): contact
[@BartoszOsiej](https://github.com/BartoszOsiej) via the channel given in
your purchase email.

## Response expectations

This is a one-person side project — community-issue responses are best
effort. Enterprise tickets are handled first.
